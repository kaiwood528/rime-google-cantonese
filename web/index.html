用英文字母輸入粵語，拼得不準也能找到想要的字 Type Cantonese with English letters, even if your spelling isn't perfect

這是 Windows 輸入法「小狼毫」的免費附加功能。你只需用英文字母拼出讀音，例如輸入 nei hou，就會出現「你好」。即使拼寫不完全準確，Google 也會協助找出你想要的字詞。回答兩個問題，即可找到最適合你的設定。 A free add-on for Weasel (小狼毫), a Windows input method. You spell out how a word sounds — type nei hou and you get 你好 (hello). If your spelling is a little off, Google's suggestions help fill the gap. 第 2 步Step 2

選擇一套拼音方案Pick a way to spell Cantonese

「拼音」是以英文字母記錄讀音的方法。兩套方案都適用，而且不少字的拼法相同，只在部分讀音的寫法上有分別。A romanization writes Cantonese sounds with English letters. Both systems work and spell many words the same; they differ only in a few sounds.

第 2 步Step 2

你的課本或課程使用哪一套拼音？Which romanization does your course or textbook use?

粵拼通常用數字標聲調，例如 nei5 hou2；耶魯拼音通常用符號，例如 néih hóu。Jyutping usually marks tones with numbers, like nei5 hou2. Yale usually uses accent marks, like néih hóu.

常見問題Questions

需要輸入聲調嗎？Do I need to type tones?

不需要。直接輸入 nei hou 就會出現「你好」。No. Just type nei hou to get 你好.

需要付費嗎？Is it free?

完全免費，並且開放原始碼。小狼毫本身同樣是免費的開源軟件。Yes, and it's open source. Weasel itself is free and open source too.

我輸入的內容會傳送給 Google 嗎？Is what I type sent to Google?

安裝時「用法」選 1（另加 Google 第 6 個候選）或 3（改用 Google 選字），會在輸入期間傳送拼音查詢；選 2 則只在你連按兩下左 Shift 時，才傳送當時正在輸入的拼音，平時不會傳送任何內容。無論哪一種，都只會傳送拼音（例如 nei hou），不會傳送已輸入的文字、視窗內容或個人資料。With usage option 1 (a 6th suggestion from Google) or 3 (Google's suggestions), the letters you type are sent as you type so they can be looked up. With option 2, they are sent only when you tap the left Shift key twice; nothing is sent otherwise. In every case only the letters (like nei hou) are sent, never the text you've already entered, window contents or personal data.

如果 Google 停止提供服務，會有甚麼影響？What if Google's service stops working?

小狼毫仍可照常使用，只是不再顯示 ☁ 候選。沒有網絡連線時亦是如此。Weasel keeps working; you just won't see the ☁ suggestions. The same happens when you're offline.

支援 Mac 或手機嗎？Does it work on Mac or phones?

目前只支援 Windows 10 和 11。Not yet. It works on Windows 10 and 11 only.

之後想更改設定或停止使用，應該怎樣做？Can I change the setup later, or remove it?

可隨時再次雙擊 setup.cmd 重新選擇，次數不限。如要移除，請雙擊 uninstall.cmd；小狼毫和粵語拼音方案會保留。Double-click setup.cmd again at any time and choose differently, as often as you like. To remove it, double-click uninstall.cmd; Weasel and the Cantonese input stay installed.

本專案與 Google 無關。☁ 候選來自 Google 輸入工具未公開的介面，Google 可能隨時更改；屆時小狼毫仍可照常使用，只是不再顯示 ☁ 候選。Not affiliated with Google. The ☁ suggestions come from an undocumented Google Input Tools service that may change at any time; Weasel keeps working without it.

候選窗僅作示意：☁ 候選是 Google 的實際查詢結果，小狼毫候選的次序則視乎詞庫及個人輸入紀錄而定。The suggestion windows are illustrations: ☁ entries are real Google results, while Weasel's order depends on its dictionary and your typing history.
