用英文字母打廣東話，拼得唔準都估到 Type Cantonese with English letters, even if your spelling isn't perfect

呢個係「小狼毫」嘅免費附加功能。小狼毫係一個 Windows 輸入法：你用英文字母拼出讀音，例如打 nei hou，就會出「你好」。拼得唔準都唔緊要，Google 會幫你估。答兩條問題，就知道點樣設定最啱你。 A free add-on for Weasel (小狼毫), a Windows input method. You spell out how a word sounds — type nei hou and you get 你好 (hello). If your spelling is a little off, Google's suggestions help fill the gap. 揀一套拼音Pick a way to spell Cantonese

「拼音」即係用英文字母寫低讀音。兩套都得，好多字拼法一樣，分別只係某啲音嘅寫法。A romanization writes Cantonese sounds with English letters. Both systems work and spell many words the same; they differ only in a few sounds.

第 2 步Step 2

你嘅課本或者課程用邊套拼音？Which romanization does your course or textbook use?

粵拼通常用數字標聲調，例如 nei5 hou2；耶魯拼音通常用符號，例如 néih hóu。Jyutping usually marks tones with numbers, like nei5 hou2. Yale usually uses accent marks, like néih hóu.

常見問題Questions

要唔要打聲調？Do I need to type tones?

唔使。直接打 nei hou 就會出「你好」。No. Just type nei hou to get 你好.

使唔使錢？Is it free?

免費，而且係開源嘅。小狼毫本身亦係免費開源。Yes, and it's open source. Weasel itself is free and open source too.

我打嘅嘢會唔會傳去 Google？Is what I type sent to Google?

「小狼毫為主」只會喺你連按兩下左 Shift 嗰陣，先將當時嗰組拼音送去 Google；平時乜都唔送。「Google 為主」要邊打邊將拼音送去查詢。兩者都只係送拼音（例如 nei hou），唔會送已經打出嚟嘅文字、視窗內容或者個人資料。With "Weasel first", the letters you're typing are sent only when you tap the left Shift key twice; nothing is sent otherwise. "Google first" sends the letters as you type so it can look them up. Either way only the letters (like nei hou) are sent, never the text you've already entered, window contents or personal data.

如果 Google 唔再提供服務點算？What if Google's service stops working?

小狼毫照常用得，只係冇咗 ☁ 候選。斷網嗰陣都係咁。Weasel keeps working; you just won't see the ☁ suggestions. The same happens when you're offline.

支援 Mac 或者手機嗎？Does it work on Mac or phones?

暫時只支援 Windows 10 同 11。Not yet. It works on Windows 10 and 11 only.

之後想改設定，或者唔想用？Can I change the setup later, or remove it?

再雙擊 setup.cmd 重新揀就得。唔想用就雙擊 uninstall.cmd，小狼毫同粵語拼音會保留。Double-click setup.cmd again and choose differently. To remove it, double-click uninstall.cmd; Weasel and the Cantonese input stay installed.

本專案與 Google 無關。☁ 候選來自 Google 輸入工具未公開嘅介面，Google 隨時可能更改；到時小狼毫照常用得，只係冇咗 ☁ 候選。Not affiliated with Google. The ☁ suggestions come from an undocumented Google Input Tools service that may change at any time; Weasel keeps working without it.

候選窗為示意：☁ 候選係 Google 實際查詢結果，小狼毫候選嘅次序視乎詞庫同個人打字紀錄。The suggestion windows are illustrations: ☁ entries are real Google results, while Weasel's order depends on its dictionary and your typing history.
